What we deliver

Nine capabilities, one team

Every engagement is scoped to your environment and budget. Below is what each service includes — mix and match, or hand us the whole stack.

Cloud & DevOps

Move to the cloud without losing control of cost, security or performance. We design landing zones, migrate workloads and automate delivery so changes ship safely.

  • Cloud architecture, assessment and migration
  • Containers, Kubernetes and platform engineering
  • Infrastructure-as-code and CI/CD pipelines
  • Cost governance, right-sizing and FinOps

Cybersecurity

Defence in depth, designed around how your people actually work. We harden, monitor and help you respond when something goes wrong.

  • Firewall, endpoint and email protection
  • Identity, access control and zero-trust principles
  • Vulnerability assessment and hardening
  • Continuous monitoring and incident response

Networks & connectivity

Networks that stay up, scale with you and keep different parts of the business properly separated.

  • LAN, WAN, SD-WAN and enterprise Wi-Fi
  • Network segmentation and secure remote access
  • VPN and site-to-site connectivity
  • Performance monitoring and optimisation

Data centre & virtualisation

Modern compute and storage platforms, plus the facilities discipline that keeps them running reliably.

  • Server, storage and virtualisation platforms
  • Data centre design, build and commissioning
  • Operations & maintenance (O&M) procedures
  • Facilities and vendor management

Software engineering

Custom applications and integrations delivered with modern engineering practice — and handed over properly documented.

  • Web, mobile and internal business applications
  • API design and system integrations
  • UX, discovery and prototyping
  • Automated testing and maintainable code

Managed IT & support

One service desk for everything — with clear service levels and engineers who already know your estate.

  • 24/7 monitoring and alerting
  • Helpdesk, incident and request management
  • Patching, updates and lifecycle management
  • On-site support and regular reporting

Backup & cyber resilience

Backups are only useful if they restore. We design, test and monitor recovery so you can prove it works.

  • Immutable and offsite backup strategies
  • Disaster recovery planning and testing
  • Defined RTO and RPO targets
  • Ransomware-resistant retention

Data & automation

Make better decisions with information that is current, consistent and easy to see — and remove repetitive manual work.

  • Dashboards and management reporting
  • Data integration and consolidation
  • Workflow and process automation
  • Reporting pipelines and data quality

IT strategy & consulting

Independent advice that connects technology spend to business outcomes — no product agenda.

  • IT health checks and maturity assessment
  • Technology roadmaps and budgeting
  • Architecture and solution reviews
  • Vendor evaluation and procurement support

Engagement models
