Questions

Frequently asked

What size of organisation do you work with?

We work with growing SMEs, established enterprises and government agencies. Engagements range from a single focused project to fully managed IT, and we scope the service to match your team and budget.

Do you only supply products, or provide services too?

Both — but our value is in the delivery. We are vendor-neutral, so we design the right solution for your environment, then implement, document and support it.

Can you support a mixed cloud and on-premise environment?

Yes. Hybrid is the normal case for most of our clients. We connect, secure and monitor workloads wherever they run, and give you one team to call.

How quickly can you respond to an incident?

Response targets are agreed in your service level before we start, so there are no surprises. Managed service clients get a direct escalation path to the engineers who know their systems.

Do you help with security and backup as well as infrastructure?

Absolutely. Security and resilience are part of every design, not optional extras — from hardening and monitoring to immutable backups and tested recovery plans.
