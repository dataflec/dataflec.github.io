Who we are

Dataflec Sdn Bhd is a wholly owned Bumiputera company on a mission to make enterprise-grade technology accessible, practical and cost-effective.

Our story

Technology that gives your business the edge

In today’s day and age, an organisation can no longer thrive without the use of technology. Our IT solutions are designed and developed in a manner that gives your business the edge over competitors.

Whatever IT challenge your organisation is facing, we work hand-in-hand with you to provide sustainable solutions to overcome it. From requirements planning and architecture through to testing, deployment and long-term support, we stay accountable for the outcome.

We also believe strongly in being part of a healthy IT ecosystem. We are committed to attracting, motivating, developing and retaining the highest-quality talent by providing a working environment that emphasises respect, integrity, honesty and opportunity.
