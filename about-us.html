We build the quiet infrastructure behind loud growth.

Dataflec Sdn Bhd is a wholly owned Bumiputera technology company. We exist for one reason: to make dependable, modern IT accessible to organisations that don’t have an army of engineers on staff.

Why we exist

Technology should be an advantage, not an anchor

Too many organisations inherit a tangle of ageing systems, mismatched vendors and decisions nobody can explain. The result is predictable: rising cost, growing risk and a business that moves slower than its market.

We take a different approach. We start by understanding how you actually work, then design around your constraints — not around a product catalogue. Every recommendation is tied to an outcome you can measure, and every system we hand over is documented enough that you are never locked in.

As a Bumiputera-owned company, we are also invested in the local ecosystem. We hire, train and grow Malaysian engineers, and we measure our success by how long clients choose to stay with us.

What guides us

Mission, vision and values

Mission

To give every organisation we work with a technology platform they can trust — secure, current and sized to their reality, not someone else’s.

Vision

To be the partner clients name first when someone asks who keeps their systems running and their data safe.

Values

Respect, integrity, honesty and opportunity — applied to clients, partners and every member of the Dataflec team.

How we behave
